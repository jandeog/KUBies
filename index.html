A stone house with a story

Built in 1830 and lovingly restored with traditional materials, Mariolata Stone House is your warm, authentic base in a quiet stone-built village, just a short drive from Parnassos and Delphi.

Authentic Heritage

Original stone walls, wooden ceilings and the character of a 19th-century Greek mountain home.

Space for Everyone

3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and two living rooms with fireplaces, comfortably hosting up to 10 guests.

Nature & Calm

A 600 m² stone yard, balcony with ravine views and the silence of the old village around you.

The House at a Glance

Upper level

Fully equipped kitchen, dining area and cozy living room with a large fireplace, plus a balcony overlooking the ravine and village.

Lower level

Three bedrooms, a second living room with fireplace and two bathrooms, ideal for families or groups.

Outdoors

600 m² stone-paved yard with a traditional wood-burning oven, BBQ, wood storage and private parking.
